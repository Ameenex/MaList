Getting started

New to Malé? Here's what to know

Before you land

Register on IMUGA at least 96 hours before landing — every passenger, including children, needs the Digital Traveller Declaration. It's fully digital now; there's no paper form. Skipping this is the most common cause of delays at immigration.

Complete IMUGA declaration →

⚠️ Watch for scam sites. The IMUGA declaration is always free on the official government portal above. Some third-party sites charge a $30–40 "processing fee" for the same free form — don't pay for this anywhere else.

Free 30-day visa on arrival for every nationality — just a valid passport, onward ticket, and confirmed accommodation. Nothing to apply for in advance.

Since 2025, holders of Israeli passports are not permitted entry under an amendment to the Immigration Act. Dual nationals can still enter on a passport from another country.

Money

Currency is the Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR). USD is widely accepted for tourist-facing spots, but Rufiyaa is genuinely useful for local shops, inter-island ferries, and street food. A currency exchange counter and ATMs are right in the arrivals hall.

Getting connected

Dhiraagu and Ooredoo SIM counters are in the arrivals hall with tourist data packages that activate instantly — one of the easiest first-hour tasks at the airport.

Culture & etiquette — please read

The Maldives is a 100% Islamic nation. Alcohol, pork products, and religious materials deemed offensive to Islam are strictly prohibited and will be confiscated at customs — even duty-free alcohol bought before departure. Dress modestly in Malé and on inhabited islands (swimwear is for resort/tourist beaches only). Public life pauses briefly around prayer times — check the live prayer chip on the home screen so you're not caught off guard.

Getting from the airport into the city

Velana International Airport sits on its own island — you'll cross a short stretch of water to reach Malé or Hulhumalé no matter what.

Public ferry

~10 min crossing

~$1.50

RTL bus R3

Airport → Malé, hourly, card only

~10 MVR

Taxi to ferry terminal

~7–10 min

~75 MVR

💳 Any contactless Mastercard or Visa card works to tap and pay on RTL buses and ferries — no local transit card or cash needed.
